# North 0.7 Guide coverage

This is the definition of complete for the member-facing North Guide. It covers what a member can see, use, input, edit, change, record, or reference. A feature is not considered explained because its button label appears in another article.

## Coverage states

- **Covered**: the Guide explains the purpose, controls, result, and any important consequence in ordinary language.
- **Partial**: a starter article mentions the workflow but does not explain all member controls or consequences.
- **Gap**: no current article explains it.
- **Verify**: publication is blocked until the claim is checked against an authoritative source.
- **Removed**: the capability is not currently exposed to members and is outside Guide scope until the product adds it.

## Verification evidence

- Health metric definitions were checked on 2026-08-04 against the official Android Health Connect data-type index: https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/data-types. North maps `COUNT_TOTAL`, `DISTANCE_TOTAL`, `EXERCISE_DURATION_TOTAL`, `ACTIVE_CALORIES_TOTAL`, and `ENERGY_TOTAL` in `HealthReader.kt`; sleep context uses the imported sleep-session interval and assigns it to the day the session ended.
- Exercise tracking, relationship references, and anatomy rendering remain covered by the canonical database validator and exercise-system audit. These checks prove structural consistency, not universal biomechanical suitability; the Guide states that limit explicitly.
- Guide index and article rendering were checked on 2026-08-04 at 390px with larger text, higher contrast, and reduced motion enabled through the member-facing Settings controls. The browser gate verifies Tab navigation, Enter activation, native accessible controls, and no horizontal overflow.
- The five-step product tour consumes `productTourGuideSteps`, the same structured steps rendered by the North in one minute Guide article. Today's muscle-map help deep-links to the structured `today-muscle-map` article and returns to the existing full-screen anatomy owner.
- Anatomy terminology, training-variable context, Health Connect semantics, and North's editorial difficulty and movement-pattern criteria were reviewed on 2026-08-04 and recorded in [GUIDE_CLAIM_SOURCES.md](GUIDE_CLAIM_SOURCES.md). The register states the exact supported claims and preserves the boundary between structural validation, editorial classification, and individual suitability.

The North 0.7 Guide publication sourcing gate is closed for the claims currently made by the Guide. Structural database validation still does not establish universal biomechanical suitability, and the member-facing copy does not claim that it does.

## Verification gates

The following content must not be presented as settled fact until its source and review date are recorded:

| Claim area | Minimum source class |
|---|---|
| Primary, secondary, and supporting muscle roles | Sourced with an explicit exercise-specific editorial limit |
| Exercise technique and safety cues | Bounded as editorial coaching prompts; diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and individualized clearance are excluded |
| Beginner, intermediate, and advanced difficulty | North's operational classification criteria are published and explicitly non-clinical |
| Push, pull, squat, hinge, carry, crawl, and rotation labels | North's dominant-intent discovery taxonomy is published and explicitly not a complete biomechanical analysis |
| Default sets, repetitions, load, duration, and distance targets | Sourced as interacting training variables; Guide explicitly presents template values as editable defaults rather than universal recommendations |
| Rest-period guidance | Sourced as goal- and context-dependent; Guide does not promise a universally correct rest period |
| Samsung Health and Health Connect metric meanings | Sourced to current official Android documentation |

Every sourced Guide article should record the source title, publisher, URL, date accessed, and the exact claim it supports. North should explain uncertainty where reputable sources disagree.

## Release rule

North 0.7 Guide completeness requires:

1. Every row above is Covered or intentionally removed from the member product.
2. Every Verify row has traceable sources and a review date.
3. Every destructive action explains its consequence before the member reaches it.
4. Every imported or generated record explains its source.
5. Every article works at 390px with large text, high contrast, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and no horizontal overflow.
6. Contextual help and walkthrough steps reuse this structured Guide content rather than maintaining separate factual copy.
